# GitHub Student Developer Pack — What You Actually Have

A quick-reference cheat sheet of the credits, tools, and limits that matter while you're coding. Keep this open so you know what to reach for and what the catch is.

---

## Cloud Budget

- **Azure** → $100 credit — good for ML, Azure Functions, cloud experimentation
- **DigitalOcean** → $200 credit — good for deploying apps and VMs, valid 1 year
- **Heroku** → ~$312 total over 2 years ($13/month) — easy PaaS deploys, no infra headaches

## IDE

- **JetBrains** → All pro IDEs free (IntelliJ, PyCharm, WebStorm, GoLand, Rider…) — renewed annually
- **Codespaces** → Cloud VS Code, no local setup needed — Pro-level access included

## CI/CD

- **GitHub Actions** → 2,000 min/month free (private repos)
- **Travis CI** → Free while student

## Auth & Backend

- **Clerk** → Auth + user management done for you, free Pro plan
- **Appwrite** → Self-hostable BaaS (auth, DB, storage, functions), free Pro plan

## Monitoring

- **Sentry** → Error tracking, free while student
- **Datadog** → Infra monitoring, 10 servers, free for 2 years

## AI

- **GitHub Copilot** → Code autocomplete, free — but model selection is limited (post-March 2026)
- **Claude Opus 4.6** → NOT in the student pack anymore
- **Claude Sonnet** → NOT self-selectable in Copilot anymore
- **Best bet** → claude.ai free tier, or check if your university has an Anthropic Campus deal

## Summary — how the Student Pack works vs Azure

Two different "free" systems again, same logic as Azure:

1. **GitHub Pro itself** — $4/month value, $0 cost as long as you're verified as a student. Quotas (3,000 Actions min, 180 Codespaces hrs, 20GB storage, etc.) reset every billing cycle and don't carry over — burn what you don't use, it's gone.

2. **The 90+ partner offers** — each one is run by a separate company with its own terms: some are time-boxed (3-6 months, 1-2 years), some last "while you're a student" (re-verified periodically), and a few (GitHub Desktop, Octicons) are just always-free tools unrelated to student status.

Your current GitHub usage ($0.43 consumed, $0.27 discounted away, $0 owed) confirms you're well inside Pro's included limits — Codespaces is your biggest live consumer (core-hours + storage), everything else sits at zero.

For your Sentinel/FinAdvisor CI pipelines: your 3,000 Actions minutes/month is the one to actually track given your CI/CD eval pipeline work — at current 0 used, you've got full headroom, but agentic GHA loops can burn through this fast if a job retries in a loop, so keep an eye on it.

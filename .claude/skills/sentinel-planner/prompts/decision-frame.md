# Decision Frame Template

When presenting a decision to the user:

```
## Decision: <title>

**What:** <one sentence — what we're deciding>

**Constraints:**
- <budget/free tier limit>
- <existing decision that constrains this>
- <technical requirement>

**Options:**

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| A: <name> | <pros> | <cons> |
| B: <name> | <pros> | <cons> |

**Recommendation:** <option> — <one sentence why>
```

Never more than 3 options. Always have a recommendation. Keep the whole frame under 20 lines.
